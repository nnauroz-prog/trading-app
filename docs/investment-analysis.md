# Concrete investment analysis

The money plan links its long-term allocation to `/geldplan/analyse`. The amount,
horizon, reserve and debt answers stay in browser storage; the server fetches only
public fund market data. A missing, unsafe or short-horizon plan exposes no
purchase quantity or buy action. The global allocation rules keep money needed
before five years in cash, consistent with this fund's issuer-recommended minimum.

The named candidate is Vanguard FTSE All-World UCITS ETF (USD) Accumulating,
ISIN IE00BK5BQT80, WKN A2PKXG, Xetra VWCE. This is one identifiable world-equity
fund, not a ranking of all funds or a forecast of future returns. Broad equity
exposure, accumulating income, costs, concentration and currency exposure are
explained in plain language. The allocation itself still uses fixed rules.

## Market and purchase calculations

Quote and history data must identify the actual EUR listing. Foreign-currency,
invalid, future or stale quotes do not produce purchase quantities. The displayed
quantity is an illustration at the last observed price, never an executable
order. Conservative cent rounding keeps whole-unit cost within the allocation;
the remainder stays unspent and broker fees have not yet been deducted. Fractional
units depend on the user's provider.

Historical returns use actual observed endpoints, with their dates shown. The
drawdown is the largest decline between earlier and later daily closing prices
in the available dataset. Missing observations do not become zero returns.
Illustrative loss scenarios are arithmetic examples, not predictions.

The optional speculative allocation links to the existing strict crypto purchase
check. Its browser-local budget is displayed there and remains unspent when no
candidate passes the purchase rules. No orders or transfers are executed.
